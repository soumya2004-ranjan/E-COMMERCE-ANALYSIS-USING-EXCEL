# E-commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard 

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing e-commerce sales data using **Microsoft Excel** to evaluate business performance, identify sales trends, understand customer purchasing behaviour, and monitor profitability.

An interactive Excel dashboard was developed using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI calculations, and multiple visualizations to convert raw transactional data into actionable business insights.

## Objectives

- Analyse overall sales and profitability.
- Evaluate monthly and yearly sales performance.
- Identify high-performing product categories and sub-categories.
- Understand customer segment-wise sales contribution.
- Analyse geographical sales distribution.
- Monitor key business performance indicators (KPIs).
- Compare annual business performance through Year-over-Year (YoY) analysis.

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Excel Dashboard
- KPI Analysis
- Data Visualization
- Data Analysis

## Dataset Information

The dataset contains **9,994 transaction records** with 22 columns, covering sales transactions from 2011 to 2014.

Key attributes include:

- Order ID and Order Date
- Customer ID and Customer Name
- Product Category and Sub-Category
- Sales and Quantity
- Discount and Profit
- Ship Mode
- State, City, and Region

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard focuses on the following metrics:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity Sold
- Profit Margin

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Monthly Sales & Profit Analysis
Analyses monthly sales and profit trends to identify seasonal patterns and business performance fluctuations.

### 2. Category-wise Sales Analysis
Compares sales contributions across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology categories.

### 3. Customer Segment Analysis
Evaluates sales performance across three customer segments:
- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

### 4. Geographical Sales Analysis
Visualizes sales distribution across different US states to identify high-performing geographical markets.

### 5. Top 5 Product Sub-Categories
Identifies the top five sub-categories based on total sales.

### 6. Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth Analysis
Compares annual sales, quantity, profit, profit margin, and order volume from 2011 to 2014.

### 7. Data Visualization
The project incorporates multiple Excel visualizations:
- Combo Chart
- Waterfall Chart
- Pie Chart
- Geographical Map
- KPI Dashboard

## Key Business Insights

- Total revenue generated was approximately **$2.30 million**.
- Total profit was approximately **$286.40K**.
- Overall profit margin was approximately **12.47%**.
- Technology was the highest-revenue product category.
- Consumer customers contributed the largest share of total sales.
- 2014 recorded the highest annual sales during the analysed period.
- Phones and Chairs were the top two product sub-categories by sales.
